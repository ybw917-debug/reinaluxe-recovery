# Assertive publication synthesis

The available intensities are `restrained`, `assertive`, and `highly_assertive_but_supportable`. ReinaLuxe defaults to the strongest level the evidence actually permits.

Restrained wording is used for narrow or single-source findings. Assertive wording is available when several scoped observations converge, including findings with a disclosed disagreement. Highly assertive but supportable wording requires corroborated independent evidence and no unresolved contradiction.

Strong market conclusions do not require a universal grade definition or factory paperwork. They do require traceable observations and a bounded inference. Visible evidence can strengthen a conclusion about visible proportions, shape, drape, construction, or comparison signals, but it cannot establish material composition, authentic origin, or universal product consistency.

The publication wording register contains all three alternatives plus the allowed and selected versions. The prohibited-overclaim register keeps the boundary visible during owner review. These are proposed enrichment candidates, not an applied article draft or publishing action.
