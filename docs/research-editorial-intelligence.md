# Research editorial intelligence

Editorial synthesis separates source observation from ReinaLuxe analysis. Every significant analyzed finding records source count, source-lane count, visible-evidence count, contradiction status, editorial inference, three wording levels, one concise limitation, a prohibited extension, proposed section, narrative value, reader usefulness, and conversion value.

For legacy reconstruction, external findings normally become `owner_analysis_of_external_evidence` or `owner_market_synthesis`. This creates original organization, comparison logic, buyer guidance, and market-level conclusions without turning another person's experience into an owner action.

Source independence uses the source domain or an explicit seller-control cluster. Repeated observations can support an assertive conclusion when independent evidence converges. A single source remains restrained; two scoped sources can permit assertive wording; corroborated independent evidence without unresolved contradiction can permit highly assertive but supportable wording.

Each section gets at most one concise scope statement. The generated wording avoids repetitive sentence-level hedging while prohibiting unsupported claims about composition, origin, universal factory consistency, receipt, handling, photography, measurement, customer outcomes, or guaranteed performance.
