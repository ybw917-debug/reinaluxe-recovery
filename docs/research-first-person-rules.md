# Research first-person rules

First-person eligibility is evidence-based, not stylistic. The plan carries explicit owner-activity flags and exports the phrases those flags permit.

Analytical activity can permit phrases such as `I reviewed`, `I compared`, `I analyzed`, `what stood out to me`, `my assessment is`, `based on the examples I examined`, and `in the discussions I reviewed`. A sourcing-conversation flag is separately required for `in my sourcing conversations`.

Physical-experience phrases have independent requirements:

- `I received the physical bag` requires a physical receipt record;
- `I photographed it` requires owner photography;
- `I weighed it` requires an owner measurement;
- `in hand it felt` requires physical handling;
- `I used it for months` requires documented long-term use;
- `my customer received it` requires an authorized client case.

Reviewing or receiving an image set does not establish receipt of a physical product. Existing legitimate first-person passages can be preserved during reconstruction, but they do not automatically authorize new physical-experience claims.
