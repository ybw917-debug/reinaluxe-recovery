# Research owner review

`research-export-review` emits the standard executive brief, plan, source and exclusion registers, atomic claims, source-to-claim map, corroboration and contradiction registers, insufficient-evidence register, image registers, topic opportunities, article opportunities, blank owner review, snapshot, and validation result.

Corroboration requires more than repeated wording: the core cluster must have evidence from at least two independent domains. Disagreement is retained as a contradiction with both positions and possible scope differences; it is not forced into a single conclusion. Single-source and otherwise insufficient clusters remain visible as `no_action` opportunities.

Owner decisions start blank. Approval, approval with changes, rejection, and deferral are versioned contract values for future decision application. The review package authorizes no drafting, shortening, WordPress action, image publication, redirect, URL change, or production-database write.
